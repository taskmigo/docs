## Planned

`entity`, `query`, `action`, `policy`, `theme`, and `navigation`.

## Known limitations and open decisions

- Multi-tenancy is not available.
- Page UI currently includes only [`Stack`](/versions/v0/ui-components/stack) and [`Typography`](/versions/v0/ui-components/typography), with their minimum props.
- Missing-translation-key behavior and translation override precedence are not defined.
- The complete MVP kind list and physical PostgreSQL layout are not finalized.
- Public revision-tag API paths, authorization, and deletion policy are not defined.

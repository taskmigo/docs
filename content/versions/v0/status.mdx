## Planned manifest kinds

- `v0/entity`
- `v0/page` and `v0/component`
- `v0/query` and `v0/action`
- `v0/policy`, `v0/workflow`, `v0/theme`, and `v0/navigation`

Planned kinds are not public contracts until they have dedicated reference pages.

## Known limitations and open decisions

- Multi-tenancy is not available: one deployment manages one Site graph in one configured PostgreSQL schema.
- Missing-translation-key behavior is not defined.
- Translation override precedence is not defined.
- The expression language beyond `Trans` and `TransN` is not defined.
- The complete MVP kind list is not finalized.
- The exact PostgreSQL table layout is not defined.
- The public schema-versioning policy is not defined.
